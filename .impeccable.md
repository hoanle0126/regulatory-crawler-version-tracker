# Impeccable Design System: RegTracker

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience:** Legal counsels, regulatory compliance officers, customs advisors, and Legal AI engineers.
- **Context of Use:** Intense desk research, regulatory monitoring, auditing legal changes across state gazettes, evaluating clause-by-clause impacts, verifying downstream Legal RAG synchronization.
- **Jobs to be Done:** Rapidly spot newly enacted, amended, or repealed provisions (*Điều, Khoản, Điểm*); compare historical versions side-by-side with high typographic clarity; export structured chunks to vector pipelines.
- **Emotional Goal:** Serious authority, calm precision, institutional trustworthiness, and intellectual focus. Absolutely zero gimmicks, zero "toy-like" SaaS aesthetics, zero AI slop.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone:** *Archival, Institutional, Razor-Sharp, Unimpressed.*
- **3-Word Personality:** **Authoritative, Archival, Precise.**
- **Anti-References:** 
  - Standard AI SaaS dashboard (dark slate, cyan glowing borders, purple gradients, pill cards inside cards).
  - Web3 / Crypto trading terminal (neon green tickers, heavy glassmorphism).
  - Generic Bootstrap / Tailwind UI admin templates with massive rounded corners and floaty drop-shadows.

### Aesthetic Direction: Editorial Legal Archive & Workstation
- **Theme:** Editorial Light (`#faf9f5` warm parchment canvas, `#1c1b18` deep letterpress ink, `#45433d` body ink, `#e5e3db` hairline rules).
- **Typography:**
  - **Editorial Display & Legal Titles:** Classic literary serif with sharp cut serifs (e.g. `Newsreader` / `Playfair` or system classical serifs like `Baskerville, 'Times New Roman', Georgia, serif` as fallback, styled with high contrast and optical tracking).
  - **Body & Legal Provisions:** Crisp, highly legible editorial serif or humanist text (`Merriweather`, `Georgia`, or high-legibility sans for compact tables).
  - **Hierarchical Codes & Checksums:** Clean tabular monospace (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace`) strictly reserved for document numbers (`01/2026/TT-BTC`), SHA-256 hashes, and statutory coordinate tags (`Chương II > Điều 5`).
- **Surface Architecture:** 
  - Flat, non-floating surfaces.
  - No rounded-2xl bubble cards. Crisp 0px or 2px corner radii.
  - Separation achieved through physical 1px hairline rules (`border-[#e6e4dc]`) and subtle tinted background panels (`#f3f1ea` / `#eceae2`).
- **Color Discipline (OKLCH-inspired tinting):**
  - Canvas: `#fbfaf7` (Warm cream / parchment)
  - Surface Panel: `#f5f3eb` (Muted paper)
  - Sub-surface: `#edeae0` (Pressed paper)
  - Ink Primary: `#181715` (Deep sumi ink)
  - Ink Muted: `#5c5950` (Aged charcoal)
  - Hairline Rule: `#e3e0d5` (Bookbinding rule)
  - Legal Added (Insert): `#ebf5ec` bg with `#1b5e20` ink (Deep forest green, not neon lime)
  - Legal Removed (Delete): `#fcedea` bg with `#8f1f1d` ink and strikethrough (Crimson ink, not fluorescent pink)
  - Legal Modified: `#fef6e7` bg with `#8c5600` ink (Warm ochre / amber ink)
  - Status Indicators: Understated letterpress tags with crisp borders, not glowing neon pills.

### Design Principles
1. **Paper Over Plastic:** Treat the screen like a prestigious legal gazette or declassified ledger. Real ink, warm paper, hairline gridlines, zero plastic glassmorphism.
2. **Every Border Must Earn Its Place:** Abolish card-wrapping containers. Use column rules, typographic scale, and whitespace rhythm to establish structure.
3. **Word-Level Fidelity:** Diff visualization must look like an editor's redline draft on parchment. Words deleted are struck through in ink; words added are underlined in forest green.
4. **Information Density with Breathing Room:** Dense tabular data where precision is required, generous column margins for long-form legal text (capped at 75ch).
5. **Radical Authenticity:** No fake AI sparkles, no floating gradients, no generic icons above every heading. High-craft, purposeful interaction.
